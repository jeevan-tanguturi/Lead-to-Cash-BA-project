# Lead-to-Cash Automation – BA Portfolio Case Study

## Overview
This case study shows how I approach a Lead-to-Cash (L2C) improvement project as a Business Analyst. It follows the full BA lifecycle: discovery, process mapping, fit-gap analysis, requirements, data analysis with SQL, UAT and governance.

The company, data and people are fictional. The methods reflect how I work on real projects.

## The Problem
Northbank Business Services sells B2B services. Its Lead-to-Cash process is slow and manual:
- Quotes are built in spreadsheets outside Salesforce
- Risk and Compliance approvals happen over email, with no audit trail
- Closed deals are re-keyed into the ERP, causing invoice errors and delays
- Some won deals are never invoiced at all

## Goal
Design a joined-up process from lead to payment, with automated approvals, a clean handover from CRM to ERP, and data checks to catch revenue leakage.

## Project Structure
- **01-discovery** – Stakeholder map, interview notes, open questions
- **02-process** – As-Is and To-Be process maps, fit-gap analysis
- **03-requirements** – User stories, acceptance criteria, traceability matrix
- **04-data** – SQL schema, sample data and analysis queries
- **05-uat** – UAT plan, test cases and defect log
- **06-governance** – RAID log, decision log, RACI

## Tools and Techniques
Salesforce and CPQ concepts, SAP/Oracle ERP integration, BPMN, Jira, SQL (SQLite), Agile/Scrum, Given/When/Then acceptance criteria, UAT
