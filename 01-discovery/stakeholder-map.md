# Stakeholder Map

| Team | What they care about | What they need |
|---|---|---|
| Sales | Closing deals quickly | No rework after signing |
| Finance | Accurate invoices on time | Deal data sent to billing automatically |
| Compliance | Following the rules | Approval before billing |
| Risk | Safe clients | Client checks completed |
| Operations | Fewer manual tasks | Alerts for failed handovers |
| Technology | Clear requirements | Defined integration rules |
