# Interview Notes

## Finance – Billing Lead
- Q: What happens after a deal is signed?
- A: Sales emails us the details and we type them into SAP by hand.
- Pain point: invoices go out 7 to 10 days late, and prices are sometimes wrong.
- Success looks like: invoices created automatically on the day of approval.

## Compliance – Controls Manager
- Q: What checks are needed before billing?
- A: Every contract must be approved, with proof of who approved it and when.
- Pain point: there is no record of approval today.
- Success looks like: billing is blocked until approval is recorded.

## Sales – Account Manager
- Q: Where do things slow down?
- A: When a deal is rejected, we don't know why.
- Success looks like: rejection reasons shown on the deal.

## Operations – Team Lead
- Q: What causes the most manual work?
- A: Chasing failed handovers between systems.
- Success looks like: a daily report of failures.
